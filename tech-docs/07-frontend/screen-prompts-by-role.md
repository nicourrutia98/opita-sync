# Screen Prompts By Role

## `requester`

Design a chat-first workspace where a requester starts from a natural-language message, receives guided clarifications, reviews extracted form fields, submits a request, and tracks case status without leaving the main workspace.

## `technician`

Design a case-oriented chat-first workspace where a technician reviews assigned work, opens case context, appends a logbook entry, and generates a controlled draft from current case context.

## `supervisor`

Design a supervision workspace where a supervisor can inspect operational progress, query status in natural language, review visible timeline data, and understand the current owner and next step of a case.

## `tenant-admin`

Design a tenant administration area integrated with the same product shell, including members, invitations, tenant defaults, theme preview, and accessibility baseline management without looking like a separate product.
