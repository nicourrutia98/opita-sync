## Baseline UX Rules

- Chat is always reachable from user-facing roles but is not the sole navigation path.
- Case detail is the anchor surface for continuity between chat, logs, drafts, and audit snippets.
- Solicitante, tecnico, and supervisor flows are mobile-first by product decision.
- Admin tenant and Opita Code governance surfaces are web-first by product decision.
- Loading, empty, error, and success states are mandatory for all critical modules in implementation.
